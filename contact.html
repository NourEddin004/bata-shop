Good to know

How much is delivery?
JD 2 in Amman and Zarqa, JD 3–4 in other cities, and free on orders over JD 60. Amman and Zarqa usually arrive the next day; other cities take 2–4 days.
Can I order on Instagram or WhatsApp?
All orders are now placed on the website, so every order is tracked with its own number and nothing gets lost in messages. Add your pieces to the cart and check out; it takes about a minute and you still pay on delivery.
Can I pay when the order arrives?
Yes — cash on delivery is the default. You can also pay by CliQ transfer, or pick up and pay at a branch.
Can I exchange a piece?
Unworn pieces with their tags can be exchanged for another size or colour within 3 days, at a branch or through the driver.
How do your sizes run?
Our cuts are loose and long. If you're between two sizes, take the smaller one. Every product page has a size guide with measurements.
Is everything opaque?
Yes. Every fabric is chosen to be non-sheer, and light colours are lined where needed.
